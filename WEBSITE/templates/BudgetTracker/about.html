About BudgetTracker

BudgetTracker is a simple web application that helps users manage their daily expenses and keep track of their money.

Our Mission

Our goal is to make budgeting easier by helping users record their expenses, manage their money, and develop better spending habits.

With BudgetTracker, users can understand where their money goes and learn how to save for their needs.
